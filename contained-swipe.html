Contained Swipe Input

This variant now uses the same tested full-screen gesture engine and wallpaper storage as the main Swipe Input trick.

Controls Swipe anywhere. In two-swipe mode: ↑←/↑↑/↑→ gives 9/0/1; →↑/→→/→↓ gives 2/3/4; ↓→/↓↓/↓← gives 5/6/7; ←↓/←←/←↑ gives 8/9/0. Double-tap clears. Quick two-finger tap submits. Swipe down with two fingers to return here; a two-finger hold is also available as a fallback.
